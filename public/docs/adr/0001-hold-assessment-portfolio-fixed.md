# Hold the assessment portfolio fixed for the 2040 comparison

The supplied data describes one financial year, while the board wants to understand how Nimbus’s climate exposure could change around 2040. Use the same nine plants and hold FY2025–26 production and direct emissions fixed so Meera can see the effect of future climate and carbon-price assumptions. A forecast of the 2040 business would require plant plans, production forecasts and decarbonisation pathways, and would combine those changes with the effects of the scenarios.
